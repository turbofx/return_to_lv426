This is a fanfic novel set in the "Aliens" universe, some number of years after the events in the "Aliens" movie.
This started out as a straight formward intelectual question:

In this universe they've got FTL, GPAI and hypersleep chambers, yet when presented with some scary unknown threat, 
their first answer is to throw squishy humans in body armor at it?

I fully realize that Aliens as a movie is a product of its time and the imagination of the people writing it. I also recognize that an element of the movie is the treatment of humans as expendable.

However, we now live in a world with drones and robotics that even in our nacient stage of development are _quite good._
I can't help myself but to ask the question of "what if?" this technology will look in a century or two.

This is my exploration into that question, while following the tropes that makes the "Aliens" universe so great.

I'm writing this in LibreOffice and then converting to PDF / TXT / HTML. I'm using Sigil to do the final EPUB
convert.

While stated in the disclamer at the top of the document, this is merely a passion project.
Please don't sue me.
