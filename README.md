This is a novel set in the "Aliens" universe, some number of years after the events in the "Aliens" movie.

This started out as a straight forward intellectual question:

In this universe they've got FTL, GPAI and hypersleep chambers, yet when presented with some scary unknown threat, 
their first answer is to throw squishy humans in body armor at it? We now live in a world with drones and robotics that even in our nacient stage of development are _quite good. I can't help myself but to ask the question of "what if?" this technology will look in a century or two.

I fully realize that Aliens as a movie is a product of its time and the imagination of the people writing it. I also recognize that an element of the movie is the treatment of humans as expendable and themes of corporate greed.

This is my exploration into that question, while following the tropes that makes the "Aliens" universe so great.

I'm writing this in LibreOffice and then converting to PDF / TXT / HTML. I'm using Sigil to do the final EPUB
convert.

If you've managed to read this far, enjoy. You may notice this repository is public! I'm very open to constructive criticism and if you find problems (which I'm sure there are plenty!) Open an issue or even better, submit a patch!

Lastly, this is a passion project that grew out of my love for the Aliens universe, please treat it as such and if you're so inclined to enforce copyrights, please don't sue me!
