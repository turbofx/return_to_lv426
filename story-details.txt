It's always bothered me how they did Aliens.

Let me get this straight. You've got spacecraft that are either going
FTL or damn close to it. You've got cryogenic chambers and you've got
computers that operate with something like a GPAI and synthetic people and
yet, the solution when dealing with an hostile alien threat is to send
human space marines to figure what happened on LV426 (Acheron)? I've
long mused about how I'd go about attacking this problem. moreover,
I've thought about what kinds of people and technology would be needed
to handle this. To that end though, just going through an accounting
of how you'd go about it is kinda boring. The protagonist is a guy who
has hidden behind technology his entire life. Loaded up with plenty of
cyberware he's a total bad ass when it comes to manipulating hardware,
but there is where his skills end. Having spent more time hitting the
books than hitting the weights, he's not "marine" material and the
contingent of marines onboard make sure to let him know it. But his
special set of skills are needed for this mission. It takes place years
after the original mission to LV426. Having learned their lesson sending
squishy humans into the jaws of xenomorph's. Despite the marines itching
to get to the surface and avenge those fellow marines left behind. Out
of reach of their corporate masters, the marines decide to disobey
orders and land on the surface. Note: The marines simply sabotage the
sub space radio. But things don't go to plan. Capturing a live drone and
bringing it aboard a sister ship constructed for holding the xenomorph
goes awry. Our hero needs to face his fears, and through courage,
guile and wits to save his friends. ==================== Vehicles
